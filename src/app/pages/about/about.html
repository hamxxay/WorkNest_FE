Our Story

Work should happen in spaces that inspire

Founded in 2026, WorkNest began with a simple idea: work should happen in spaces that inspire. What started as a single shared office space has grown into a network of premium workspaces across major cities.

We noticed that traditional office spaces were no longer meeting the needs of modern professionals. Freelancers, startups, and remote workers needed flexibility, community, and environments designed for focus and creativity.
